Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Lockdown is a consumer digital-security application in pre-launch development. This Privacy Policy explains how Lockdown handles information when users connect supported services for security monitoring.

Information Lockdown may process

Depending on the service a user chooses to connect, Lockdown may process account identifiers, authentication tokens or app-specific credentials, mailbox metadata, mailbox folder information, mailbox-rule or mailbox-behaviour indicators, and security-notice metadata such as sender, subject and date.

Yahoo Mail data

If Yahoo Mail access is approved and enabled, Lockdown intends to use OAuth 2.0 with read-only IMAP access for user-requested mailbox-security monitoring. Lockdown may process the minimum mailbox metadata needed to detect suspicious mailbox behaviour and relevant security notices.

Lockdown does not use Yahoo Mail access to send email, delete messages, modify mailbox settings, build advertising profiles, or sell mailbox data.

How information is used

  • To provide mailbox-security monitoring requested by the user.
  • To compare current mailbox behaviour with previously observed or trusted state.
  • To detect potentially suspicious mailbox activity or security-related changes.
  • To generate security alerts and incident-history records.
  • To diagnose service errors and maintain the security of the service.

Authentication and security

Lockdown is designed to avoid storing normal account passwords where provider OAuth is available. Authentication tokens and supported mailbox credentials stored on the backend are encrypted. Device authentication is used to protect access to Remote Watch data.

Data sharing

Lockdown does not sell personal information or mailbox data. Lockdown does not share mailbox data with third parties for advertising, marketing profiles or resale. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers only where necessary to operate the service securely.

Data retention

Lockdown aims to retain only the information needed to provide security monitoring, maintain user-requested history, and operate the service. Retention practices may be refined before public launch as the product and compliance programme mature.

User control

Users are expected to be able to disconnect monitored accounts and revoke provider authorisation. Provider-side authorisation can also be revoked through the relevant account provider.

Children

Lockdown is not designed or directed for use by children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Lockdown develops, adds providers, or changes its security and data-processing practices. The latest version will be published at this location.

Contact

For privacy or security questions relating to Lockdown, contact the developer using the contact email supplied in the relevant provider application or developer-access request.
